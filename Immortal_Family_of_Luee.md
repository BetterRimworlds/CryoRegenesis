# The Immortal Family of Lucee

## The Ring

One day, Lucee's family discovered a giant ring artifact, buried in an ancient mine.

It was a large ring, nearly ten meters tall, bearing thirty-nine glyphs and one
six-digit arrangement carved into solid silver. They did not know who had made
it, where it led, or whether it had ever been intended for human hands. They
applied power, manually dialed the glyphs on the ring, and watched as a blue
vortex swooshed open.

Then they bravely walked through.

All of them.

On the other side they found an abandoned Atlantean outpost somewhere in the
Milky Way. The place was ancient but not dead. Buried beneath its silent rooms
was an Ancients laboratory complete with a databank, several depleted
Zero-Point Modules, and machinery built by a civilization whose science had
long ago escaped the boundary between technology and miracle.

The family did not immediately return home. They stayed for twenty-seven years.

## The Twenty-Seven Years

For twenty-seven years they studied the outpost and reverse engineered what
they came to realize was a Stargate. They learned its language of symbols, its
address system, its safeguards, and eventually enough of its power systems to
make the outpost their own.

Among the outpost's treasures was a sarcophagus that regenerated the living.
It repaired ruined bodies, reversed the damage of age, and allowed a family to
remain together beyond any natural human lifetime. Nearby were several
depleted ZPMs. The family could not reproduce them, but they learned to charge
them slowly from their own electrical system. What the Ancients had powered
effortlessly, Lucee's family sustained through patience, industry, and an
almost unreasonable refusal to let the old wonders die.

This was the foundation of Lucee's immortal family.

Lucee, her husband, and her children, Lucy and Luci, ceased to measure family
history in ordinary generations. Their lives stretched across planets and
centuries. Even Cargo, their beloved Husky, remained beside them. He crossed
world after world with the family and eventually celebrated his 165th
birthday — a very good dog who had lived longer than nations.

The sarcophagus did not make their existence effortless. Immortality had to be
maintained. Power had to be generated, machines repaired, uranium secured, and
every irreplaceable piece of Ancient technology protected. Survival ceased to
mean enduring one winter or one raid. It meant building systems that could
continue for centuries.

## The Planetkiller Expeditions

For more than a hundred years, the family has traveled temporarily through the
Stargate, always into the past and usually to around the year 5500 — the beginning
of so many RimWorld stories.

They choose planets scheduled for destruction by Planetkillers.

To most people, these worlds are doomed homes. To the family, they are also
finite operations with an absolute deadline. Every expedition begins with the
knowledge that the planet, its settlements, and everything left upon it will
soon be erased. The family stripmines each planet for all it is worth. Ore,
components, machinery, weapons, medicine, knowledge, animals, and people are
moved through the gate while there is still time.

They also take on anyone who joins them.

As the final six months of the planet's life approach, rescue becomes conquest.
The family viciously attacks the tribes, using Zat'nik'tels whenever possible
to take the maximum number of prisoners. The tribal peoples are like the
Earthlings in *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*: many probably do not even
know that their planet will die in a few months, and none possess a way to
escape it.

If the family conquers all the tribes in time, they move on to the cities. If
the cities fall and the clock still permits it, they attack the pirates.

The method is violent, but the approaching Planetkiller makes every choice
morally crooked. People are assaulted, captured, and displaced by the same
family that carries them away from certain extinction. Lucee's family does not
wait for every doomed society to understand, consent, organize, or believe
them. They impose survival first and leave judgment for the decades that
follow.

## The Penal Worlds

Everyone captured during a Planetkiller expedition is moved to one or more
penal colonies on other, newer Planetkiller planets.

There they serve sentences of five to ten years, stripmining worlds whose
destruction lies farther in the future. The mines supply the family network
with the metals, uranium, components, and construction material needed to keep
the Stargates active, charge the ZPMs, extend the Glitterroads, and sustain the
sarcophagi.

The penal colonies are not forever worlds. Their deadlines are simply more
distant. Every mine, prison, power plant, and settlement is built with eventual
evacuation in mind. The prisoners labor beneath the knowledge that the ground
itself has an expiration date — and that completion of their sentence offers
both release and passage off the doomed planet.

Those whom the family comes to trust, admire, or love may remain with them and
travel onward to the next Planetkiller. Those who do not are released onto one
of the family's Urbworlds after serving their time.

No one is left behind when the end comes.

## The Forever Worlds

The permanent centers of the family network are Forever Worlds: stable planets
transformed over decades into Urbworlds.

Every city is connected by Glitterroads that the family has largely built or
extended themselves. Stargates join worlds across distance and history.
Lantean transporter rooms join the cities, mines, storehouses, hospitals, and
gate facilities of each planet. What began as one abandoned outpost has become
a dispersed civilization whose roads and transporters make whole worlds feel
like districts of a single metropolis.

The Urbworlds hold the things that cannot be risked on a Planetkiller:
centuries of records, family homes, Ancient databanks, sarcophagi, charged and
charging ZPMs, breeding populations, strategic reserves, and vast stores of
Luciferium.

They are not merely refuges. They are proof that Lucee's family can do more
than escape destruction. Given enough time, they can turn scattered RimWorld
cities into a connected planetary society.

## The Luciferium Compact

Lucee, Lucy, Luci, and Lucee's husband are all Luciferium addicts by choice.

It gives them the zest to remain productive, to keep moving, and to continue
finding more Luciferium. Over the centuries they have accumulated vast stores,
especially on the Forever Worlds. Their immortality rests on more than the
sarcophagus: it rests on logistics, surplus, and a red medicine that can never
be safely abandoned once accepted.

Released prisoners are purposefully addicted to Luciferium when they are
settled on an Urbworld. They fill the cities by the hundreds and can eventually
make up twenty to fifty percent of the World Pawns.

That demographic change reshapes the economy of an entire RimWorld.
Luciferium is no longer an exotic demand belonging to a handful of desperate
people. It becomes a staple traded among cities because a huge portion of the
world requires it. Settlements acquire it, caravans carry it, and markets keep
it in circulation. The result is a self-reinforcing economy in which
Luciferium is almost always plentiful in most cities, increasing the family's
supplies and helping guarantee the survival of everyone already bound to it.

The compact is both social engineering and mutual dependence. The family gives
former prisoners a place on a surviving world and access to an economy capable
of sustaining their need. In return, their collective need makes that economy
too large and too persistent to disappear easily.

## The Secret Empire

Lucee's family has built an empire.

It has defined territory: Forever Worlds, Urbworlds, Atlantean outposts, penal
colonies, gate facilities, mines, cities, roads, and the space between them.
What makes it unusual is not the absence of borders, but the secrecy of the
infrastructure that binds those borders together.

The empire is built upon a Stargate network that only the Initiated know how
to use — or, in most cases, even know exists. To an ordinary citizen, caravan,
visitor, or rival government, its settlements may appear impossibly distant
and only loosely connected. The Initiated know that a guarded room beneath one
city can open onto another planet in seconds. Theirs is an experience much
like that of the Tau'ri in *Stargate SG-1*: the greatest strategic fact in
human history remains hidden behind ordinary doors, classified knowledge, and
a circle of people trusted to understand what the ring can do.

This secrecy gives the family an advantage more decisive than any army.
Enemies must cross interplanetary space; the family crosses a threshold.
Resources extracted from a dying planet can appear on a Forever World without
a transport fleet. Soldiers, prisoners, animals, medicine, and entire
households can vanish from one world and emerge on another before outsiders
understand that a departure has taken place.

The empire therefore has two maps. One is the map the galaxy can see: isolated
colonies and distant settlements separated by years of travel. The other is
the true imperial map known to the Initiated: a web of adjacent places, each
one Stargate address away.

## The Shattered Empire and the Spice

For more than two hundred years and across dozens of planets, the family kept
its deepest technology within that secret civilization. The CryoRegenesis
quests have begun to change this.

The family has now made its regeneration technology known to the Shattered
Empire. Nobles have learned that the stories are real. The family's
Glittertech can heal ruined bodies, reverse age, and keep the Empire's
leadership Forever Young. Even the Emperor will visit soon enough.

This is the family's "spice".

CryoRegenesis is the only viable path by which the Shattered Empire's
leadership can remain Forever Young. The Stargates are the only means by which
those leaders can travel instantly between worlds. The family does not need
to conquer the Shattered Empire to become indispensable to it. It possesses
the two things that an ancient ruling class cannot obtain elsewhere: more life
and more time.

That knowledge creates a new and dangerous phase in the secret empire's
history. Each visiting noble sees only a fraction of the truth, but each
successful regeneration makes secrecy harder to preserve. The family must
decide who may know about the gates, who may be taught to dial them, and
whether the Shattered Empire is becoming an ally, a client, or a rival waiting
to understand the value of what it has found.

## The Governance System

The secret empire is governed through two parallel orders of rank. The first
is political and establishes formal precedence among rulers, commanders, and
regional governors. The second is philosophical and metaphysical, recognizing
those whose lived endurance makes them Elders of the civilization.

Neither order wholly replaces the other. Political rank determines who holds
office and exercises state authority. Elder rank determines who commands
reverence and whose judgment carries the greatest moral weight. A person can
stand very high in one order and much lower in the other.

The system relies upon three measurements, but the measurements are tools of
government rather than titles by themselves:

- **Chronological Age** is the time elapsed since birth, including centuries
  spent in cryptosleep.
- **Biological Age** is the present physical age of the body.
- **True Age** is the life actually experienced, preserving years erased from
  the body by regeneration while excluding unconscious years in cryptosleep.

Together they allow the empire to distinguish between someone who has crossed
history, someone who has consciously lived through it, and someone whose body
has survived it.

### The Political Order

Formal political hierarchy is based primarily upon Chronological Age. The
most ancient stand highest because they connect the empire to lost eras, dead
worlds, and the origins of its civilization. Below them, offices such as
military leader and regional Countess define practical areas of command.
Ancient standing establishes precedence; appointed office establishes
jurisdiction.

Cryptosleep counts fully within this political order. A sleeper may not have
experienced every passing year, but they survived the passage of history and
awakened in an age that once lay beyond imagination. Their very presence
creates continuity between worlds and centuries that would otherwise have
nothing living in common.

Anna Smith, née Jacobs, was born in 2027 and is by far the most ancient member
of the empire chronologically — perhaps even the most ancient person in the
galaxy. She is a living link to ancient Mother Earth, born millennia before the
Shattered Empire and nearly every institution that now claims authority. In a
civilization where continuity across time is the root of political
legitimacy, no pedigree is older than hers.

She is married to the chief architect Theodore Smith, born in 4464 and now
1,062 years old chronologically. Theodore — namesake of the chronicler of this
history — has spent approximately one thousand years in cryptosleep. That makes
him a Methuselah Sleeper.

Theodore's second wife is Diana Singleton Smith, the regional Countess. Born
in 2064, Diana is 3,462 chronological years old. Anna and Diana have each
crossed roughly three times as much sleeping time as Theodore. Their marriages
therefore unite military command, regional nobility, and living memory of an
almost unimaginably ancient Earth.

Methuselah Sleepers are people uprooted from their native centuries who
endured long enough to become rulers and citizens of the distant future.
Their title is not a claim that sleep equals lived experience. It recognizes
their political role as bridges across historical time. Anna and Diana have
each crossed roughly three times Theodore's sleeping interval, giving all
three great standing while preserving the wives' far deeper chronological
precedence.

### The Elder Order

The Elder Order confers a different kind of rank. It values True Age in
relation to Biological Age and natural Life Expectancy. Its highest members
need not hold the largest territory or command the strongest army. They are
revered because they have demonstrated the most difficult trait to prove on
the Rim: long-term survivability.

An Elder is not merely someone born long ago. An Elder has remained alive,
awake, and physically old. A person pushing 150 biological years is presumed
to have led a very safe and survivable life, regardless of Chronological Age.
The body itself becomes evidence of judgment, resilience, caution, medical
discipline, and an ability to endure dangers that destroy almost everyone
else.

Standing within the Elder Order is expressed approximately as:

`Elder Merit = True Age / (Life Expectancy / Biological Age)`

Equivalently:

`Elder Merit = (True Age × Biological Age) / Life Expectancy`

The formula rewards two accomplishments at once. True Age represents the life
actually experienced. Biological Age represents how far the body has been
allowed to travel along its natural course without relying upon rejuvenation
to remain young. Life Expectancy normalizes both values against the person's
species.

Lucee, matron of the family, is 187 years old biologically and has a True Age
of approximately 250 to 257 years. She has spent only a few dozen years in
cryptosleep. Lucee has chosen rejuvenation only during extreme medical
emergencies, and only to the extent required to survive deadly plagues and
similar threats.

Using a True Age of 250 years, a Biological Age of 187, and a human Life
Expectancy of 80:

`250 / (80 / 187) = 584.38`

Theodore has a True Age of 62.82 years and a Biological Age of 56.18:

`62.82 / (80 / 56.18) = 44.15`

By this system, Lucee possesses more than thirteen times Theodore's Elder
Merit.

### Authority in Practice

There is no contradiction in Theodore exercising military command while Lucee
stands thirteen times higher in Elder Merit. Theodore's office gives him
command of the armed forces. Lucee's standing makes her the more revered
Elder, the person whose life most strongly embodies the civilization's ideal
of survival.

The two orders are meant to restrain and complete each other.  A Methuselah Sleeper
will awaken with enormous political rank but modest Elder Merit. A younger-born 
person who remains awake for centuries and accepts rejuvenation only when death is 
otherwise certain may become one of the most revered beings in the civilization. 
The first has crossed history. The second has lived it. Both have a place in the 
governance of the secret empire, but they do not occupy the same tier or wield the 
same kind of authority.

## The Family Across Time

Lucee's family rules a secret empire with territory spread across the
Milky Way and across time. Its domain is a chain of stargates, outposts, penal
worlds, Forever Worlds, cities, and people. The territory appears discontinuous 
only to those who do not know how to read its true map.

Which is basically everyone in a civilization that assumes it would take 
dozens of years to get from planet to planet at relativistic speeds.

Their mission: Covertly expand their stargate network, work up the Noble ranks
on planet after planet, have entire generations of the Shattered Empire's nobility
owing them their last 200+ years of life, and eventually stage a silent coup.

That purpose has survived for more than two hundred years, but it has grown
large enough to determine the fates of entire planets. The family rescues by
conquering, imprisons in order to evacuate, addicts in order to reshape
economies, and destroys settlements on worlds already sentenced to
destruction. It offers continuity in a universe built around catastrophe, but
the price of that continuity is control.

To some, they are raiders who arrive from nowhere with impossible weapons.
To others, they are jailers who stole five or ten years and then delivered them
to planets that would take 100s of years to get to. On the Urbworlds they are 
founders, road builders, keepers of the gates, and the ancient family whose 
private refusal to lose one another became the foundation of civilization.

And at the center of it all are still Lucee, Lucy, Luci, Lucee's husband, and
Cargo The Immortal Pup: the family who found a burried Stargate, dialed six glyphs, 
saw a blue vortex swoosh open, and — all of them — bravely walked through.
